Free AI Tools for Students

Find useful AI tools for studying, research, writing, notes, presentations and student productivity — all in one place.

Choose an AI tool based on your task

Students use AI for many different tasks. Instead of choosing a tool just because it is popular, use this directory to find tools that match what you actually need to do.

How to choose the right AI tool for studying

For research: Look for tools that help you find, understand and organise information rather than simply generating an answer.

For writing: Use AI as an editing and brainstorming assistant. Always review the final result yourself.

For notes: Tools that can work with your own documents or study material can be especially useful for revision.

For presentations: Choose a tool that can help turn your ideas or notes into a clear visual structure.
